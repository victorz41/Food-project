The Problem

Millions of people go to sleep hungry and most restraunts and stores throw away good food every day. It's already bad that stores are not giving left over food to those in need they are throwing it in landfills that rot and produces a huge amount of methane gas that is a key gas contributing to the global warmings problem.


☕

Our Mission

Our mission is to try to make sure that less people go to bed with a hunger. FoodBridge is a website that allows restraunts and stores to use their leftover food for a good purpose. FoodBridge allows stores to donate leftover food in catagories and donate them to nerby food shelters,churches, and homeless shelters. Our goal is to make less people sleep with an empty stomach and with food that would have been wasted and thrown in to landfills.


🍽️

Our Target Audience

Our main targets are

  • Restaurants and Grocery Stores: Reduce waste and contribute to social good.
  • Food Shelters and Charities: Receive consistent food donations.
  • Individuals in Need: Access to surplus food through local organizations.
